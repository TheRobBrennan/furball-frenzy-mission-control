# Character trait ledger

The cast is built as the game grows. Every trait, personality quirk, strength, interest, habit, and
relationship Rob mentions, even in passing, lands here with the date, and moves into
`apps/api/app/crew.py` once it is confirmed. Public text stays at "North Carolina" and "a fleet of
vehicles and a big property", no town, street, owners, or acreage.

Status: **confirmed** means Rob said it, **proposed** means I suggested it and he has not
confirmed it, so it is never presented as canon.

## How to add one

Append a row to the character's table with the date, the detail, and the status. If it is
public-safe and confirmed, add it to `crew.py` in the same change.

## Game ideas, proposed

- **The binder is the game's navigation.** The elephants' tabbed binder, colored dividers and all, becomes the main UI, one tab each for the Brief, Research, Missions, Parts, Journal, and Debrief, so the research and journaling they are proud of is the thing the player flips through. The journal pages are the mission debrief. *Proposed, from Rob's binder idea.*
- **Piggy borrows their devices.** He already winds up at the helm of the iPad Pro and the MacBook Pro, with their own, he can borrow the elephants' and teach them what the buttons do. *Proposed.*
- Keep the binder generic, a tabbed 3-ring binder, no brand marks or logos on the public site.
