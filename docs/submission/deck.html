Furball Frenzy:
Mission Control

Two Polish elephants, one lifelong dream: shining in the American Space Program. Design the mission that gets them to the Moon.

A team of one, with a crew of curious and playful furballs.

The crew, window seats

The challenge

Design, manage, and simulate a mission, and learn why it worked or failed

  • A mission objective, then vehicle, instruments, power, and communications
  • Six resources shown clearly: mass, power, budget, data, communications, time
  • Every decision has a consequence, and there is no single perfect design
  • Feedback that explains why, and replay with changes

Our answer: a game anyone can play in 30 seconds on a phone, with a story, not a spreadsheet.

2 / 7
Design

Pick the parts, watch six gauges

  • A launcher, instruments, a solar array, an antenna for the elephants' rover to the lunar south pole
  • Gauges update on every click, and the launch button explains why it is locked
  • Judges, start here: the elephants design and launch a mission in about 30 seconds
3 / 7
Launch

The mission is a story, with surprises

  • A go or no-go at the final checks, the crew fixes it, then 3, 2, 1, liftoff
  • Random trouble in space: alarm, calm, Piggy's tearful photo, then repair or a safe turn for home
  • A splashdown countdown, and everyone is home safe, paws, trunks, snouts, and tails
  • Sound, timestamps in UTC and local time, like a real mission log
4 / 7
Learn why

A debrief that teaches

  • What cost the most, and what was left on the table
  • Your rover compared to a real NASA surface mission: Sojourner, Spirit and Opportunity, Apollo LRV, VIPER, Curiosity, Perseverance
  • Figures read from NASA pages, linked, and the game says “not stated by NASA” when a page gives none
5 / 7
How it is built

Tested, simple, and live

  • A pure TypeScript simulation, 170 unit and component tests, Playwright flows at phone and laptop width, Storybook stories
  • Next.js, React, Tailwind, FastAPI, Docker, behind a Cloudflare Tunnel on an Oracle VM, every merge deploys
  • A frozen copy at a tagged commit for judging
  • Mobile first, reduced motion respected, privacy page, analytics only with consent
  • Built with Claude as the daily coding collaborator, said openly
